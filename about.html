About Us

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2018 with a simple mission: to bring authentic, slow-fermented artisan bread back to our local community. What started as a modest home kitchen passion project quickly grew into a beloved neighborhood fixture. Guided by a dedication to traditional baking techniques and early morning craftsmanship, we opened our storefront to share the comfort of freshly baked loaves and pastries with our neighbors every single day.

From our very first batch of sourdough to the hundreds of pastries that leave our oven each morning, our promise remains unchanged: quality ingredients, time-honored methods, and a warm welcome for everyone who steps through our doors.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that exceptional baked goods start with honest, carefully chosen ingredients. We build strong relationships with regional farmers and producers who share our commitment to sustainability, quality, and care.

  • Locally Milled Grains: We source stone-ground, non-GMO flour from regional grain mills to support local agriculture and preserve rich, natural flavors.
  • Real Farm Dairy: Our butter, milk, and cream come from regional dairy farms, ensuring our croissants and brioche have an authentic, rich taste.
  • Seasonal & Whole Ingredients: We use real fruit preserves, unrefined sugars, and fresh herbs. You will never find artificial preservatives, food colorings, or synthetic flavorings in anything we bake.

Staff Highlights

With over 15 years of artisan baking experience, Elena leads our bread production every morning. Her deep passion for natural sourdough starters and long fermentation techniques forms the heart and soul of North Star Bakery's signature loaves.
